# ⚡ Electric Vehicle Market and Performance Analysis

Interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard for analyzing 2,000 electric vehicle records, exploring pricing trends, driving range, vehicle specifications, drivetrain distribution, and market segments using PivotTables, PivotCharts, and interactive slicers.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard developed to analyze the Electric Vehicle (EV) market using 2,000 vehicle records.

This project explores important aspects of the EV market, including vehicle pricing, driving range, drivetrain distribution, market segments, and customer ratings. Using interactive visualizations, KPI cards, and slicers, the dashboard helps identify patterns and compare different vehicle categories.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze EV market data and identify patterns in:

- Analyze average vehicle prices across different body types.
- Examine driving range trends by year and brand.
- Understand the distribution of EVs by drivetrain type.
- Analyze the distribution of vehicles across different market segments.
- Explore customer ratings and overall EV market performance.
- Support better data-driven decision-making through interactive visualizations and filters.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes the following KPIs:

- Total EV Records
- Average Vehicle Price
- Average EV Range (Miles)
- Average Customer Rating

## 📊 Dashboard Features

The interactive dashboard includes:

- **KPI Cards:** Display Total EV Records, Average Vehicle Price, Average EV Range, and Average Customer Rating.
- **Average Price by Body Type:** Compares average EV prices across different vehicle categories.
- **Average EV Range by Year:** Shows changes in average driving range across different model years.
- **Average EV Range by Brand:** Compares the average driving range of different EV brands.
- **EV Count by Drivetrain Type:** Displays the distribution of vehicles across AWD, FWD, and RWD.
- **EV Records by Market Segment:** Visualizes the distribution of EV records across Budget, Luxury, Mid-range, and Premium segments using a Treemap.

### Interactive Features

The dashboard includes four interactive slicers:

- Vehicle Brand
- Model Year
- Vehicle Body Type
- Drivetrain Type

All connected dashboard visuals update dynamically based on the selected filters, allowing users to explore different aspects of the EV market.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- **Vehicle Pricing:** Trucks had the highest average EV price, while SUVs had the lowest average EV price.
- **Year-wise Range Analysis:** 2021 recorded the highest average EV range at approximately 276 miles, while 2023 recorded the lowest at approximately 257 miles.
- **Brand-wise Range Analysis:** Honda recorded the highest average EV range at 326 miles, while Xiaomi recorded the lowest at 224 miles.
- **Drivetrain Distribution:** AWD accounted for the highest share of EV records at 38%, while FWD accounted for 28%.
- **Market Segment Analysis:** The Premium segment had the highest number of EV records, with 712 records, while the Budget segment had the fewest, with 151 records.

## ⚠️ Limitations

- The analysis is based on the available 2,000 EV records.
- The project mainly focuses on descriptive analysis of EV market data.
- The dashboard does not predict future EV prices or driving ranges.
- The accuracy of the insights depends on the quality and completeness of the dataset.
